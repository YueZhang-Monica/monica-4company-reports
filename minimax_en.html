Stage readout

01Where MiniMax stands当前阶段判断

We break the organizational evolution of AI model companies into four stages: tech validation → productization → scaling & commercialization → listed maturity. This framework is not an industry standard — it is an analytical lens derived from the HR and JD data in this study.

Our read: scaling & commercialization, with one foot already in listed maturity.

MiniMax listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on January 9, 2026 (0100.HK, raising ~HK$5.54B, closing day-one at a HK$90.9B market cap). Conventional wisdom says hiring slows after an IPO and the organization shifts to consolidation — yet its open roles nearly equal its total headcount, and corporate functions (finance / government relations / investor relations) are being built out systematically. Its chosen path: put IPO capital directly into headcount expansion, not consolidation. Among the four companies, MiniMax has the highest open-role count (461) and is the only "already listed + still expanding at scale" combination.

Overseas: the real battlefield since day one生意主场从第一天就在海外

Overseas revenue accounts for >70%, while the overseas business team is only ~30 people — per-capita output that leads the four companies by a wide margin. Key tension: listed ≠ steady state关键张力：已上市 ≠ 稳态

IPOs usually signal consolidation, but MiniMax is doing the opposite — 461 open roles say management treats listing as the starting line of expansion, not the finish line. The company is in a "high-throughput" state where headcount, roles and reporting lines are all shifting quickly. The next organizational moves will necessarily be: keep stacking technical scale domestically (Infra + elite algorithm talent) while completing listed-company corporate functions.

AI native organization

03AI Native organization: why "incomplete" is a different paradigm为什么"看起来不完整"其实是另一套范式

a) Talent density vs. scaled execution人才密度驱动 vs 规模化执行驱动

Big Tech HR systems assume "scaled execution" — work breaks into standard actions, people are hired and managed in bulk. AI Native companies assume talent density: every researcher is a high-value individual, and organizational output depends on the caliber of a few key people, not the headcount. The HR mission shifts from "hiring at volume" to "deep acquisition and retention of a small number of key talents".

1

A 48-person HR team carries 461 open roles — HR headcount tracks "key-talent acquisition volume", not total employee count;

2

A senior hiring lead was brought in externally in June 2026, covering functional-line recruitment — matching precisely the systematic emergence of listed-company corporate roles;

3

Heavy intern / campus roles coexist with a dedicated "AI Talent Partner" role — talent acquisition is front-loaded to the campus stage and given a dedicated owner.

Talent strategy

04Talent strategy: who, how to select, at what price要什么人、怎么选、怎么定价

4.1 Scale & nature of expansion: HR scales first — building the machine, not adding handsHR 先扩，且扩的是"机器"不是"人手"

Of the 36 HR professionals on Maimai, 11 joined between January and July 2026 — roughly 30% expansion; on LinkedIn, overseas recruiters were deployed in four cities over the same period. This is not backfilling; it is two proactive investments: domestic poaching (all from organizations with mature recruiting systems) + overseas outposts (supporting the already-steady ~30-person overseas business team). On Boss Zhipin, 6+ HR-team roles are visible — HR hiring HR means the expansion expectation has entered organizational preparation, a leading indicator that runs ahead of press releases.

4.5 Outflow × expansion: read the loop together流失与扩编放在一起读

Out: executive layer — product head Zhang Qianchuan transitioned to advisor in 2024, strategic-investment head Wei Hantong left in July 2025; HR layer — Zi Wang → DeepSeek, Tristan Zhao → RED (Xiaohongshu), Cheng Hongping promoted to HRD at 记忆张量, Ariel Yang → Happy Elements.

In: over the same period — 30% HR expansion, overseas outposts in four cities, 461 open roles fully opened.

Reading the loop: departures cluster in product, strategic investment and HR — non-core research layers; no named losses among core research / algorithm backbones. Outflow and inflow together: after the IPO, role adjustments and non-core-layer mobility are allowed, expansion plus liquid equity offsets attrition, and all resources are bet on the next round of technical-scale expansion. This is not bleeding — it is a rotation of the guard.
