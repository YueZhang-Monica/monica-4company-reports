Our read: scaling & commercialization (deep phase), and the only top-tier AI model company whose expansion is driven by IPO capital.

Business mix: localized delivery defends the base, MaaS and Agent are the growth engines本地化交付守基本盘，MaaS 与 Agent 是增长引擎

The 2025 annual report gives a clear directional sense: localized model revenue RMB366M (+70.5%), 50.5% of total revenue — ToB delivery is still the base; cloud/API revenue RMB190M (+292.6%) and agent revenue RMB166M (+248.8%) together take 49.2%, growing roughly 4× the localized pace — the ToB delivery model is migrating toward MaaS. Read: a lightweight overseas model of "Beijing HQ runs the globe, only business vanguards abroad, no overseas HR posts" — there is a time lag between internationalization roles being posted and people landing, not a contradiction.

Key tension: market-cap pricing > fundamentals市值定价 > 基本面

A HK$200B market cap against RMB724M revenue and RMB4.7B net loss — the recruiting expansion is supported by the market cap, and the cost-sensitivity phase has not arrived. If the STAR Market IPO cadence changes, the sustainability of expansion is in question; the fundraising VP's departure during IPO preparation is an early risk signal on capital-ops continuity. What this means for the organization: this is a company "buying time with money" — within the window, moves will be extremely dense, but the window itself depends on capital cadence. The next organizational moves will necessarily be: thickening the Agent product line + regional sales and delivery continuing to roll out + one more expansion round after the STAR Market listing lands.

AI native organization

03AI Native organization: why "incomplete" is a different paradigm为什么"看起来不完整"其实是另一套范式

a) Talent density vs. scaled execution人才密度驱动 vs 规模化执行驱动

Big Tech HR systems assume "scaled execution" — work breaks into standard actions, people are hired and managed in bulk. AI Native companies assume talent density: every researcher is a high-value individual, and organizational output depends on the caliber of a few key people, not the headcount. The HR mission shifts from "hiring at volume" to "deep acquisition and retention of a small number of key talents".

1

60% of the 46-person HR team are fresh graduates — graduates stack execution volume, a few key veterans manage key talents, and the limited senior quota is bet on the key lines;

2

Management is extremely thin — only 2 senior managers, no recruiting-director / TA-Head layer in between, and the top HR role may report straight to a business VP or the CEO line;

3

HRBPs themselves post "HRBP [LLM — recruiting + BP direction]" roles on Boss — "recruiting + BP" written into the JD title is the most direct evidence of HR = recruiting as one;

4

First-hand exchange with Zhipu HRBP Sakina (2026-07) — BPs must also deliver recruiting; there is no large standalone TA center. HR's core value is "the glue", which requires "technical taste" and staying 0.5–0.7 steps ahead of the business.

4.1 Scale & nature of expansion: HR scales first — and what it builds is the systemHR 先扩，且补的是"体系"

After precise filtering from the employer side on Boss, the current HR/recruiting team is 46 people: 4 senior managers/leads + 18 HRBPs + 5 recruiters + 14 specialists/assistants + 3 interns + 2 OD/culture, of whom 60% are fresh graduates and ~18 are experienced HRs. Organizationally this is the classic "HR = recruiting as one + HRBP-led" model — no large standalone TA center; HRBPs do both BP and recruiting. HR expansion is still ongoing: on Maimai the HRBP team is hiring its own HRIS specialist, HRSSC specialist and HRBP (algorithm team / commercialization team / R&D direction). The formation timeline is clear — 2021 Xiaoxiong/Edith → 2022 Sam/Shelly/Sif → 2023–2024 Hannah/Sakina (international background) → 2026 fresh graduates — growing from a "domestic execution squad" into an "internationalized + premium" structure. HR hiring HR means the expansion expectation has entered organizational preparation, a leading indicator that runs ahead of press releases.

4.5 Outflow × expansion: executive refresh, stable research layer, ecosystem hedge高管换血、研究层稳、生态化对冲

Out: executive layer — COO Zhang Fan left in late June to start a venture (AI Agent direction; the new project joined the Zhipu MaaS ecosystem with Zhipu investment support), fundraising VP Zhang Kuo left in late January; HR layer — Zhang Mingxi → AgiBot, Yangyang Qian → Sand.ai.

In: over the same period — 200+ open roles fully opened, HR keeps self-expanding, Tsinghua joint postdoc recruitment expanded, base-model pay stays firm.

Reading the loop: departures cluster in the commercialization / capital-ops executive layer; no named losses in the core research layer — multiple stability signals (departure rumors officially denied, joint postdoc expansion, firm base-model pay). The most interesting part is the hedge: the COO's departure was converted into an ecosystem investment target — "depart without draining", using investment ties instead of employment ties, rare among expansion-stage companies. Outflow and inflow together: this is not bleeding — it is re-betting resources on the next round of Agent and commercialization expansion.

Takeaway

Betting public-market money on the deep water of Agent and commercialization: among 200 open roles, Algorithm 55 + Agent 44 + Infra 38 + Commercialization 26 + Corporate Functions 37 form the full five-line picture — read its organization through the five-line chain, evaluate its HR through the talent-density paradigm, and spot the people it wants via "experience tiering + pipeline front-loading".
