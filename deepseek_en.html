Its first ¥51 billion funding round lands,
and the first thing DeepSeek doubles is its own HR team

Bleeding and transfusing at once — its expansion logic can't be measured with a conventional growth-company yardstick

After the June 2026 first funding round (~¥51B / ~$7B, ~$52B post-money) broke the long-held "no fundraising" stance, the HR team grew from ~17 to 32 people within two months, and this new team now carries ~250–280 open roles deduplicated across three channels; on one side, ~10 core researchers departed by name, on the other, Agent productization and the finance function were built from 0 to 1. This brief unpacks it along four frameworks: what stage it is in, what game it is playing, how the organization is shifting, and what kind of people it needs.

Our read: S2 → S3 transition, scaling driven by the first funding round.

The read rests on three independent pieces of evidence: role mix shifting from research to product (Agent is the largest hiring direction), an application inflection point has appeared (Agent's share of both role count and pay confirms it), ~10 named departures of core research talent; plus four same-source signals (total role volume / level strategy / geography / pay), all pointing to rapid expansion. Strategy

02Strategy: the corporate catch-up game after the funding lands融资到账后的"公司化补课"棋局

Driver: the first round is a strategic turn, not just money首次融资是一次战略转向，不只是钱

Liang Wenfeng long held to a "three no's" stance (no fundraising / no IPO / no selling technology) — this round broke it. Management has effectively admitted publicly: scaling has been placed above the independent research culture. The action sequence after the money landed is very clear: in May 2026 (before the round closed) the HR team began expanding — the front-running itself is a signal of funding certainty; the round closed in June and expansion accelerated; in July, 9 senior recruiters landed together and all three channels scaled up in sync, with the media phrasing "plans to at least double the size of every department" (media citation; no official announcement text seen); around the same time, FT / SCMP reported a new funding round kicking off (target valuation toward $70B, unconfirmed officially).

Overseas: nearly absent, and dual-channel confirmed出海：几乎缺席，且是双渠道确认的

Across the three domestic channels we see only one HR-adjacent role in New York (an NYU student, joined in June); LinkedIn searches for US / UK / Singapore surface no verifiable overseas business roles either, and the only three people with genuine overseas links are all HR or technical roles. Verdict: DeepSeek is currently a purely domestic research entity with no overseas business team — this is "dual-channel cross-confirmation", not "unobserved in a single channel". The New York HR role is internationalization testing the water, not a systematic deployment.

Key tension: the research-culture moat is exactly the productization drag研究文化的护城河，恰是产品化的阻力

DeepSeek's research culture is rare among AI model companies: no KPIs, no attendance tracking, a founder who says publicly that "overtime is a waste of compute", and executives writing code alongside the rank and file (public information). This culture attracts top research talent, but it works against the engineering discipline productization requires — and the main battlefield of this expansion round is exactly Agent productization and consumer-grade product building (11 frontend/client roles, a dedicated emotional-intelligence data PM role, AGI management trainees reserved for new business lines). Whether the organization can grow delivery discipline while keeping its research culture is the real exam of this S2 → S3 switch.

AI native organization

03AI Native organization: a research company where HR is the first engine一家"HR 即第一生产力"的研究型公司

a) Talent density vs. scaled execution人才密度驱动 vs 规模化执行驱动

Big Tech HR systems assume "scaled execution" — work breaks into standard actions, people are hired and managed in bulk. AI Native companies assume talent density: every researcher is a high-value individual, and organizational output depends on the caliber of a few key talents, not the headcount. The HR mission shifts from "hiring at volume" to "deep acquisition and retention of a small number of key talents". DeepSeek is the most extreme sample of this paradigm — a small, elite research layer (100–499-employee company size), yet recruitment density already in the industry's first tier.

1

HR doubles before the business: at least 15 HR joined between May and July 2026 (including 5 with 10+ years), growing the team from ~17 to 32 — the gap left by departing researchers is filled with "professional HR who can help hire even more people";

2

The recruiting org is sliced along technical chains: each of the 17 recruiters owns one technical line (dedicated owners for Agent Infra / core-system inference / frontier research / data + IDC / Code Agent data) — poaching and outreach must be matched to the right person per direction;

3

One HR team across two entities: High-Flyer Quant and DeepSeek share an HR team (several members took the High-Flyer → DeepSeek path) — under the talent-density paradigm, HR resources follow "the entity where the key talent sits", not the legal entity.

Talent strategy

04Talent strategy: who, how to select, at what price要什么人、怎么选、怎么定价

4.1 Scale & nature of expansion: a veteran bench built in two months两个月"速成"的资深班底

At least 15 HR joined between May and July 2026: May–June were mostly backbones plus newcomers; July was the densest with 9 people, and the veterans concentrated there (all three 10+ year hires landed in July) — Katrina Chen (ex-ByteDance executive recruiting → head of technical recruitment at Bilibili's main site), Yin Tingting (ex-AWS / Tencent Cloud), Peng Xiao (ex-Tencent senior hiring manager + South China campus recruitment and university-enterprise cooperation manager) started posting roles in bulk within weeks of joining; the 232-role Maimai board was mainly carried by this cohort in July. Supply-side context: Big Tech reorganizations are releasing mature HR mid-level talent in batches (Tencent HR downsizing, Baidu HR cut from ~500 to under 300, ByteDance's BP/recruiting system restructured after the ads business split) — DeepSeek's fast build-out is a supply-demand resonance between Big Tech-released HR mid-levels and AI companies' capitalized expansion, and this poaching window will not stay open forever. A second piece of evidence: DeepSeek openly recruits for its own HR on Boss / Maimai (recruiting manager 20–40K · 16-month pay) — HR hiring HR means the expansion expectation has entered organizational preparation.

4.5 Outflow × inflow: read the bleeding and the transfusion together失血与输血放在一起读

Out: ~10 core researchers departed by name (official acknowledgement-list basis: "nearly 300 people → 10 have left"): Wang Bingxuan → Tencent, Luo Fuli → Xiaomi, Guo Daya → ByteDance Seed, Ruan Chong → DeepRoute.ai, Wei Haoran — destination undisclosed. Every poacher is a direct competitor on the same track; the talent war is a zero-sum game. Market sources say competitor offers were 2–3× (unconfirmed). Overall attrition is ~5%, and Baidu-related teams have been directionally absorbing its core talent.

In: over the same period — net HR inflow of 15, ~300 open roles across three channels fully opened, top-paying research internships laying the pipeline; on the inflow side, ByteDance Seed lost ~70 technical people over the past year, some flowing to DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and others (a talent-flow signal shared across multiple companies).

Reading the loop: DeepSeek runs an atypical "bleeding while transfusing" model — 10 irreplaceable researchers leave, backfilled with 30 engineers + 15 HR, i.e. "offsetting individual attrition with organization-building". Whether it works depends on the substitutability of research output — no verdict yet. But it has one attrition buffer no other company has: its models have become the standard component of competitors (Tencent's AI strategy includes plugging DeepSeek models into applications built on top) — talent loss is partially offset into an industry-influence premium.

Takeaway

05One-line takeaway一句话结论

Using the first round to make up the corporate catch-up: ~10 core researchers bleed out, the recruiting machine doubles first (HR 17→32), then ~300 roles across three channels carry Agent productization + finance from 0 to 1 + top-tier pricing for the research elite — read its organization through the four-line chain, evaluate its HR through the talent-density paradigm, and spot the people it wants via "engineering taste + top-pay pipeline".
