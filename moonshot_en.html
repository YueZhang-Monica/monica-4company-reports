"Stability" is more likely a pre-IPO equilibrium of waiting for options, not cultural stability. Referencing peer experience at listing milestones, talent mobility structures shift significantly around IPO events. The next organizational moves will necessarily be: Infra to fill compute gaps, growth to fill the narrative, finance to fill compliance — all before the listing milestone.

AI native organization

03AI Native organization: one channel, two layers, an invisible business team一个渠道、两层分工、一支不露面的业务团队

a) Talent density vs. scaled execution人才密度驱动 vs 规模化执行驱动

Big Tech HR systems assume "scaled execution" — work breaks into standard actions, people are hired and managed in bulk. AI Native companies assume talent density: every researcher is a high-value individual, and organizational output depends on the caliber of a few key people, not the headcount. The HR mission shifts from "hiring at volume" to "deep acquisition and retention of a small number of key talents". Moonshot AI pushes this paradigm to its most extreme expression at the channel level:

1

Single-channel monopoly — Boss Zhipin 0 roles (deliberately abandoned China's largest hiring platform), LinkedIn nearly zero HR footprint, all open roles concentrated on Maimai: precisely targeting "passive candidates within the AI circle", avoiding mass resume screening, pursuing precision targeted poaching;

2

Hiring density 26.3% — Maimai shows 278 employees vs. 73 open roles, one open role for every four employees: this is not conservatism, it is a "few channels × high density" precision play;

3

Two-layer division + invisible business side — posting and decision-making are separated: posting is entirely concentrated at the execution layer, while decisions sit with the senior layer who post very few roles; business leaders are completely invisible on hiring channels, with all recruitment operated by the senior HR layer.

4.5 Inflow × outflow: the other side of zero attrition零流失的另一面

Out: no reports of any core technical personnel or management departures found; founding team intact (Yang Zhilin / Zhang Yutong / Zhou Xinyu / Zhang Yutao all in position), HR team also shows zero departure records.

In: 91 open roles at high density, HR senior layer expanded by 7+ people in H1 2026.

Reading the loop: the "zero attrition + high hiring" combination should not be read as "the organization has no problems", but rather as filling capability gaps, not replacing people: the large-scale hiring of growth/commercialization talent is filling the capability gaps needed for the IPO narrative. At the same time, referencing peer experience at listing milestones, an IPO significantly changes talent mobility structures — the current "stability" will be taken over at the listing event. Moonshot AI's inflow-outflow loop is essentially a time-stamped loop.

Takeaway

05One-line takeaway一句话结论

Betting all the IPO-window money and moves on the "growth story": among 91 open roles, Infra accounts for 28.6%, Agent 23.1%, Commercialization 19.8% — read its organization through the five-line chain, evaluate its HR through the talent-density paradigm, and spot the people it wants via "senior poaching + hybrid-role pricing". Stability has an expiration date.
